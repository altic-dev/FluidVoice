# Simple controlled microphone-admission recordings

Make five separate FluidVoice meeting recordings. Each should last roughly 20–30 seconds and test only one condition. Exact timing is unnecessary.

Use the currently installed app and your normal microphone settings. Use speakers if that is how the false captions occurred. For playback tests, use the same speech-heavy podcast or video each time.

## Recording 1 — playback only

1. Start the podcast or video through your speakers.
2. Start a new FluidVoice meeting recording.
3. Stay completely silent for about 20–30 seconds.
4. Stop the recording.

## Recording 2 — normal speech, no playback

1. Stop all playback.
2. Start a new recording.
3. In your normal voice, read this twice:

   “The blue lantern is beside the window. I will review the document after lunch.”

4. Stop the recording.

## Recording 3 — quiet speech, no playback

1. Keep playback stopped.
2. Start a new recording.
3. In a naturally quiet meeting voice, read this twice:

   “A small silver key rests under the green notebook.”

4. Stop the recording.

## Recording 4 — normal speech over playback

1. Start the podcast or video through your speakers.
2. Start a new recording.
3. In your normal voice, read this twice:

   “The blue lantern is beside the window. I will review the document after lunch.”

4. Stop the recording, then stop playback.

## Recording 5 — quiet replies over playback

1. Start the podcast or video through your speakers.
2. Start a new recording.
3. In a naturally quiet voice, say these phrases with a short pause between them:

   “Yes.” “Not yet.” “I agree.” “Please continue.” “That is correct.”

4. Repeat the five phrases once, then stop the recording and playback.

## When finished

Tell Codex:

- approximately when you completed the five recordings;
- whether you used speakers or headphones;
- whether anyone else spoke nearby;
- whether any recording differed from the instructions.

Codex will locate the five local sessions and analyze them offline. No recording will be sent to an external reviewer. These are development recordings; separate held-out recordings are still required before enabling suppression.
