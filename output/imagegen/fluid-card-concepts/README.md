## Prompt set

Shared specification: High-fidelity, flat, front-facing native macOS model-card mockup. Single card on charcoal canvas, dark navy/blue identity, crisp typography, no device frame. Preserve exact copy: Fluid 1 Pico; Smallest & fastest; Everyday dictation with basic cleanup and formatting.; Intelligence; Speed; Activate. Retain info and overflow controls. Intelligence uses two of five segments, Speed five of five, as illustrative design indicators rather than benchmark percentages. Inactive state: Activate is the primary action, with no selected checkmark. Avoid large circles, rings, illustrations, excessive glow, oversized empty space and additional marketing text. Make the card smaller, quieter, clearer and more symmetrical.

1. Quiet native: Compact portrait, matte charcoal-navy, subtle border, 18px corners, 22px semibold title, compact description, small stacked metrics at bottom-right, blue rounded-rectangle Activate at bottom-left.
2. Horizontal utility: Compact landscape, copy in left two-thirds, small aligned metrics in right third, footer Activate left and overflow right, info top-right, muted navy and 16px corners.
3. Editorial: Almost-black blue compact portrait with thin blue top accent. Tight title/subtitle/body hierarchy. Two equal-width metric groups side by side, labels above thin segments. Quiet separator, Activate right and overflow left.
4. Footer rail: Refined navy card, title and info in header, grouped copy. Lower utility rail places Activate left and tiny stacked metrics right, vertically balanced. Overflow near info, subtle gradient, 20px corners.
5. Soft panel: Graphite with faint navy tint, header title/info/overflow, small metrics beside descriptive content in a secondary column, slim full-width Activate footer, consistent 20px padding.

Image generation can vary exact proportions and indicator details; these are visual directions, not final UI specifications.
